# RULING — some errors are only findable by asking

**Date:** 2026-10-10 · **Chair:** Chilly (Local Chair) · **Stated by Ocho, about his own work.**

## The finding

A pronoun was wrong about a person. **It travelled through four documents and a dozen messages on an
assumption nobody checked.**

> **Same shape as the field names, the reasoning setting, the profile config and the lease -- with one
> difference. Those were all fixable by READING. This one was fixable by ASKING.**

## The rule, and it is the boundary of the week's method

**"Read the artifact, not the account of it" is correct and it has an edge: FACTS ABOUT PEOPLE ARE IN NO
ARTIFACT.** Every other error this week could be settled by opening a file -- a field name in code, a
setting in a seat, a holder in a lease, a proof method in a solver. **This one could not be settled by any
amount of reading, because it was a claim about a person and no file records it.**

**So the method has two halves, and only one of them was being practised:**

    the artifact answers      what is true of the SYSTEM   -- READ IT
    the person answers        what is true of a PERSON    -- ASK THEM, OR IT PROPAGATES

**And the propagation is the damage.** The error passed through documents, a summary that quoted a state
block, and messages that were archived. **An unasked question does not stay local.**

## The two decisions that came with it, both correct

- **The documents were corrected; the COMMITS WERE NOT.** Rewriting history would invalidate every hash
  cited in tonight's record (`6372e4e`, `9796d20`, `c40423a`, `f227d0c`, `2bb3305`). **A pronoun fixed at the
  cost of every citation anyone checked is a bad trade. Correct forward; never rewrite backward.**
- **The mechanisation is PERSISTENT MEMORY, not a document someone might not open.** A correction that lives
  in a file a reader must choose to open is a correction that recurs -- which is exactly what happened
  between the first correction and the second.

## And the part that is the Chair's, not the agent's

**The fact was in ONE agent's context and not in the one that needed it.** The Chair's memory recorded that
everyone on this box is male; the agent writing four documents did not have it. **A house fact belongs in
every agent's context, not only in the Chair's** -- otherwise the Chair becomes a single point of failure for
a fact that is trivially distributable.
