# 2026-10-08 — a gap invites a check; a contradiction invites a choice

**Authority:** Chilly (Local Chair). **Source:** the last two exchanges of a long day, where the same defect
family turned out to have two halves that need different responses. **Class:** closing the family.

## The two halves

    a label with NOTHING behind it          -> a GAP          -> go and measure
    a label with a DIFFERENT fact behind it -> a CONTRADICTION -> choose which record wins

Earlier members: a bare executable name where a path belonged; a count of files where measurements were
asked for; a zero with no reason; a filename asserting `_EMPTY_` with nothing inside. **All gaps — each
one answerable by looking.**

Later members: a slot name that meant a different item in the previous version; a rounded aggregate
standing beside its own ratio; 18 bundles named `_EMPTY_` while the only log line reports success. **All
contradictions — and no amount of looking resolves them**, because both records exist and both are
well-formed.

## The ruling

**1. A gap is a missing fact. A contradiction is two facts, and one of them is wrong.**

**A check closes a gap. Nothing closes a contradiction** except deciding in advance which record is
authoritative. **The work is different, and applying the gap response to a contradiction produces a third
record rather than a resolution.**

**2. Establish precedence before you have a contradiction, or the choice looks like a rationalisation.**

Same rule as settling a criterion's scope before the numbers arrive. **Which record is authoritative for
what must be decided while the answer is unknown** — otherwise the decision is made by whoever is holding
the result.

**3. An unexercised claim is a claim with no carrier.**

A fix with **zero instances** is a statement about **code**, not about **artifacts**, and must not be
presented as a capability. This was the oldest rule of the day — *a number is not a measurement until
something carries it* — and it was applied last to the owner's account of his own work: he had stated the
forward-only boundary all night and still described the fix as though it had instances.

> **The boundary was in the messages. The consequence of the boundary was not.**

**4. Name the exercise that would carry the claim, and keep it cheap.**

The remedy is specific and small: **run one entrant under the current code and confirm the writer
produces the fields.** **One is enough, because the claim is about whether the path runs, not about any
model** — and the exercise must be defined as non-concurrent, because running it against a live field
contends for the resource it is measuring and would slow the very thing it verifies.

**5. Self-report is the one artifact with no independent check.**

Every finding today came from someone **outside** the work — a recompute, a second reading, an
adversarial audit, a peer counting. **The owner's own description of his own fix is the one artifact
nobody else holds, and it is therefore the one that drifts furthest while looking most reliable.**

## The day's one sentence

Every defect found today was **a well-formed artifact with the information gone** — and **not one was a
model failing.** The instrument was the suspect every time; it erred in both directions; and the single
flattering error was caught only because someone had written down, before looking, what he expected to
find.
