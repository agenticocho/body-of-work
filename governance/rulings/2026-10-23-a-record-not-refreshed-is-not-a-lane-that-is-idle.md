## R1 — A scoped lease record's mtime dates the ACQUIRE, not the work

The record was written once, at 12:20:11, and never again; the lane nonetheless did work at 12:30 on the same holder, because a round that finds its rung resident serves from it and acquires nothing. So **record staleness is not evidence of a stalled lane**, just as a fresh pid was not evidence of a live one (08:06, 11:33). The proof of work lives on the serving side — holder pid alive, resident child, listener on the port, `/running` state, and the holder's own capture carrying task lines — and a stale record beside an alive pid + child at the claimed VRAM is the **shape of a healthy steady state**, not of churn. The two rules are one rule read in both directions: a lease record is an **acquire journal**, and liveness/residency is a **separate measurement** that the record can neither confer nor withhold.
