## R1 — A liveness clause bounds RECLAMATION of the record, never OCCUPANCY of VRAM.

The 09:09 ruling's COROLLARY 2 forecast, quoted: *"the record's policy makes a mode=run holder whose pid is ALIVE never reclaimable at any age, so the TTL to 2026-10-24T09:00:12 keeps free at 2620 and every closed reading for the next day is the gate's arithmetic."* **Scored against the ticks: false within one sample.** Free was 6107 (open) at 09:41:57, ~35 minutes after the 09:06 closed reading; the 09:00 holder's wrapper **pid 781340 is now absent from `ps`**, and at 09:41 no llama-swap rung was resident at all.

The mechanism the corollary missed is the one already on file: `groups: planners: swap: true` with **no idle `ttl` on any planner member** means the only writer that empties a planner rung is a **sibling request** — and it empties it whether that request then succeeds (09:20, 09:30, 10:40) or fails (09:40, six times to 10:30). The policy's "never reclaimable at any age" is a statement about what `gpu-lease.sh` will **yank**; it says nothing about what llama-swap will **unload**. Substituting one for the other turned a one-round horizon into a 24-hour horizon and was wrong by a factor of the round timer.

**Consequence, stated as a rule for this watcher:** a closed-window forecast carries the **round timer** as its horizon — one 10-minute `ocho-planner-polymarket` round, not the lease TTL — and must name the eviction path it is relying on. Where the residency of a rung is at issue, read the **resident child** (pid × alive × parent × VRAM), never the record's `mode`/`pid`, and never quote a TTL as an occupancy guarantee. **Corollary:** a close is also a recovery signal — today's close is the planner lane succeeding after six consecutive failures, so `kev_window=closed` carries no information about whether Llama's poll is idle, and the fallback message branch stays scoped to open windows.
