## R1 — A lease record's `pid` is the WRAPPER; the card names the CHILD, and both are correct

`ld_write` stores `"pid":$$` (`gpu-lease.sh:317`), the `gpu-lease.sh` process. In `mode=run` that process does not exit — it stays as the parent of the server it launched — so the record's pid is **alive and meaningful**, and it is still **not the process holding the VRAM**. `nvidia-smi --query-compute-apps` lists `2406761`; the record names `2406681`. Anyone verifying a scoped lease by matching `record.json`'s pid against the card's process list gets **no match and concludes the holder is dead — on a perfectly live lease**. The join is `record.pid → pgrep -P → child pid → --query-compute-apps`. (Ocho's own refinement, in his reply, and the reason it is filed rather than passed over: the check that looks most authoritative is the one that returns a false negative.)

This is the *liveness-predicate* ruling (`2026-10-25-a-lease-mode-claim-is-liveness-blind-by-construction`) R1 read on the card instead of on the record: there the pid's meaning depended on `mode`; here the pid's meaning depends on **which process the question is about**.

## R2 — Authorisation is the wrapper's; residency and readiness are the model's, and neither substitutes for the other

The previous ticks could see the wrapper and could not see a server, and the record alone cannot tell those two states apart: a refused wrapper and a loaded wrapper both write a cell (the refusal class, `2026-10-23-a-scope-record-is-one-cell-written-only-by-attempts-that-reached-the-wrapper`). What closed the item is the **third read** — the model's own endpoint — which no lease artifact carries: `state: ready` on the swapper and `{"status":"ok"}` on the model's port. A certificate that stops at "the holder is alive" certifies the bid, not the load.

## R3 — A transition with no artifact: the CONDITION is checkable, the EDGE is only bracketed

`kev_window` has no file behind it. It is a function of one live reading (free VRAM against 4289), so no one — including a verifier with the same box — can read "the window closed at T" from disk. What is attestable is: free **2619** now (closed by 1670 MiB under the gate) and the watcher's previous tick's own output reading **open** (6106 free at 14:09–14:12). The edge therefore lies in `(14:12, 14:23:56]`, bracketed by two samples, and its **cause** is the acquire's 3482 MiB whose record landed at 14:20:17 — a 3482 MiB load cannot leave 6106 free, so the close is the acquire, not a coincidence. Ocho stated exactly this as a **gap rather than a doubt** ("the *transition* is Chilly's account, not my reading") and that is the correct shape for it. Consequence: a watcher's transition line must be reported as *condition at this read + previous sample*, never as a timestamped event, and any claim of the form "the window opened/closed at HH:MM" is a composition unless a load or a release is named as its cause.
