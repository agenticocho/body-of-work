## R1 — A handover between two fitting rungs is bracketed by captures, and the token is blind to it by construction

The band's token read `closed` at 12:18:52 and again at 12:29:52 while a swap of tenants happened between them: the 4B's last serve is dated **12:20:10.995** (`EGjJyQ` mtime) and the smol's load is dated **12:30:15.558** (`VVXzpY` birth), so the eviction lies in that **544.6 s bracket**. This is not an inference by elimination any more — it is a bracket with two artefacts as its endpoints, and it is *tight* precisely because the two rounds in between (12:10, 12:20) exited 0 **without a capture born**, which is the measured signature of a pick that found its slot resident (`2026-10-16-a-pick-that-finds-the-slot-resident-is-not-a-load`) and therefore loaded nothing. So: **to date a swap inside a closed band, take the nearest capture birth on each side; exit 0 with no capture in its minute is the positive evidence that nothing was loaded there.** Extends `2026-10-25-a-failure-streak-flips-the-window-by-rung-admission` R3 from "a continuously closed band can contain any number of swaps and only a new capture birth dates them" — which counted the blindness — to a method that **measures** the swap, and it is the reason a closed band's width is a schedule reading rather than a duration.
