## Rulings

**R1 — a silent consumer leaves a GAP in the pick log, and the gap is the certification.** `pick_model_slot()` increments one counter (`ocho_sandbox/model_cycle_counter.txt`) that is consumed by the planner round AND by every shadow-planner subprocess whose pick line lands in no log, so `pos = cycle % 10` is a congruence over the SUM of consumers. Measured this tick, both directions: the logged sequence is **gapless 58165→58169** (the earlier gap 58162–58164 was the 07:00 shadow run's three calls) **and** `model_cycle_counter.txt` read **58170 == last logged pick + 1** BEFORE the 08:00 call ⇒ no unlogged consumer had run since 58169, so the head rung was certified from the log line (58169 % 10 = 9 = smol) and the next rung was decidable in advance (58170 % 10 = 0 = 9B) — and it landed. Rule: the integrity of a rung calendar is measured by the CONTIGUITY of the logged pick sequence plus `counter == last_pick + 1`; a gap marks cycles whose rung is unknowable, and a prediction spanning a gap is a claim about who else calls the planner, not about the rotation. Read the cycle from the LOG LINE; the counter file holds the post-increment value (58171 after the 08:00 round).

**R2 — the transition bracket is readable from the tick ledger, not inferred from cadence.** `cron/executions.db` shows this job's previous run at 07:47:06 (`completed`, 1.4 s, `delivery_outcome=suppressed`) and this run at 07:58:06 (change detected 07:58:07.584).
